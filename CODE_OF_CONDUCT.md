# Code of conduct

Participants in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces are expected to be respectful and constructive. Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code with specific, actionable feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content, and sharing another person's private information without consent are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to maintainer Faraazuddin Mohammed at mohdfaraaz1@gmail.com. Reports will be handled with discretion; information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban repeated or serious offenders. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.
